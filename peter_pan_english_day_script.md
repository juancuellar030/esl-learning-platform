## SCENE 1 - Wendy's Bedroom
*(ambient music or sound 1)*

**WENDY:** Shh... everyone's asleep. I love telling stories before bed.

**PETER PAN** *(whispering, just arrived)*: *(opening window sound effect)* Psst! Wendy!

**WENDY:** Who's there?

**PETER PAN:** It's me - Peter Pan!

**WENDY:** Peter Pan?! You're real?!

**PETER PAN:** Of course I'm real! I came back for my shadow... and I heard your stories through the window.

*CHIME - Campanita* *(fairy sparkle sound effect)*

**WENDY:** Who was that?

**PETER PAN:** That's Tinker Bell. She's a little jealous, so be careful.

*CHIME (short, annoyed)*

**PETER PAN:** See? Told you.

**WENDY:** Where do you live, Peter?

**PETER PAN:** Neverland! Where children never grow up. Want to come?

**WENDY:** Neverland... Can I bring my brothers?

**PETER PAN:** Sure! Just think happy thoughts... and believe.

---

## SCENE 2 - Flying to Neverland
*(ambient music or sound 2)*

**WENDY:** I'm flying! I'm really flying!

**PETER PAN:** Follow me! Second star to the right, and straight on till morning!

*CHIME - Campanita sprinkles pixie dust* *(fairy sparkle sound effect)*

**WENDY:** It's beautiful up here!

**PETER PAN:** Welcome... to Neverland!

## SCENE 3 - Meeting the Lost Boys
*(ambient music or sound 3)*

**SARA:** Someone's coming! Everybody hide!

**MIA:** Wait - it's Peter! He's back!

**LUCIANO:** And he brought someone with him!

**LORENZO:** A new friend! Come on, come see our hideout!

**SAMUEL:** Hi, I'm Samuel! Welcome to our hideout!

**PABLO:** I'm Pablo. Are you going to stay with us?

**MATIAS:** This is the best island in the whole world, you'll see!

**WENDY:** Hello, everyone! I'm Wendy. Peter says you need someone to tell you stories.

**LOST BOYS** *(together)*: Yes! Please, please, please!

**PETER PAN:** See, Wendy? Everyone loves a good story here.

---

## SCENE 4 - The Fairies' Warning

**EVA:** Psst... Wendy... watch out.

**SAMANTHA:** The pirates come this way sometimes.

**DANNA:** Captain Hook never stops looking for Peter.

**ANTONELLA:** He wants revenge for his hand!

**BRIANA:** A crocodile ate it - and now it follows him everywhere!

**FAIRIES** *(together)*: Tick... tock... tick... tock...

**PETER PAN:** Don't worry, Wendy. I'm not afraid of Hook.

---

## SCENE 5 - The Pirates Arrive
*(ambient music or sound 4)*

**CAPITÁN GARFIO:** Where is that insufferable boy... Peter Pan!

**LAURA BERNAL:** Captain, we found footprints near the lagoon!

**ALEJANDRA PRO:** Fresh ones, Captain - someone's close.

**ERMES:** Should we search the whole island?

**PAULA V:** I heard children's laughter over there!

**SANTIAGO OTÁLORA:** It must be that Pan boy and his little gang.

**JUAN SARRIAS:** What's the plan, Captain?

**DANIEL:** We could set a trap by the cove!

**CAPITÁN GARFIO:** Excellent idea, Daniel! Set the trap - Peter Pan will be mine at last!

**PIRATES** *(together)*: Aye, aye, Captain!

---

## SCENE 6 - The Confrontation
*(ambient music or sound 5)*

**PETER PAN:** Looking for me, Hook?

**CAPITÁN GARFIO:** Peter Pan! At last!

**PETER PAN:** You'll never catch me - you never have!

**CAPITÁN GARFIO:** This time is different, boy!

*(ticking clock + crocodile walking and growling sound effect)*

**CAPITÁN GARFIO:** That sound... no... not the crocodile!

**PETER PAN:** Sounds like your old friend is hungry, Hook!

**CAPITÁN GARFIO:** Retreat! Everyone, retreat to the ship!

**PIRATES** *(together, running off)*: Aye, Captain! Run!

*(running away sound effect)*

---

## SCENE 7 – Ending
*(ambient music or sound 6)*

**WENDY:** We did it! Neverland is safe again!

**LOST BOYS** *(together)*: Hooray for Peter Pan!

*CHIME - long, happy chime from Campanita*

**PETER PAN:** Wendy, will you stay a little longer?

**WENDY:** I wish I could, Peter... but my brothers and I need to go home.

**PETER PAN:** Then I'll fly you back myself. Second star to the right...

**WENDY:** ...and straight on till morning.

**ALL CAST** *(together)*: The End!

*(the end sound effect)*
